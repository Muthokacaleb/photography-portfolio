Behind The Camera

Caleb Muthoka

NAIROBI, KENYA

I'm Caleb Muthoka, a Nairobi-based photographer interested in people, atmosphere and visual storytelling.

My work sits between photography, design and culture. I enjoy finding the moments that happen naturally — the expression before the pose, the movement between scenes, and the details that make an experience feel personal.

Whether shooting in high-contrast urban light or quiet interior sanctuaries, the focus remains absolute: authentic connection and pristine visual delivery.
